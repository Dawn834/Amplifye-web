Leadership Team

John Melo

/ CEO

John G. Melo is an accomplished entrepreneur, CEO, and board director with over 30 years of global synthetic biology and energy industry experience.

Prior to joining the company as CEO, John was instrumental in the industrialization of synthetic biology, a science that involves bioengineering organisms for industrial production. Raising over $2.2B in funding and achieving a successful IPO with peak equity value of over $5B, John achieved revenue of over $650M annually from zero - and established a business model for monetizing molecules produced by genetically engineered microorganisms, bringing to market over 14 molecules that are currently consumed by over 400 million consumers daily and represent over the above industrialist in their respective end markets.

John has held numerous executive positions at Fortune 500 companies, most recently serving as President of US Foods. At US Foods he had P&L responsibility for fast marketing, supply chain and premium products business. Respected inside JP as an innovative leader with a track record for solving and troubleshooting the biggest & most complex problems inside the company. Established leader with deep understanding of business metrics, complex supply chains, product development and M&A integration.

John is fluent in both Portuguese and English and is highly regarded as a writer and speaker. He is frequently quoted in major business publications on topics covering on industrial biotechnology, sustainability, and beauty brand building, health and wellness, food safety and digital marketing.

JJulie Hirsch, PhD

She has a deep knowledge of technologies & ingredients, formulations, processing, quality, clinical operations, consumer research, regulatory. As a leader, she has a passion for hiring and motivating talent.

Wilson Mak, PhD

/ Co-Founder & CTO

Wilson Mak is an enzymologist and chemist, with over a decade of specialized experience in the realms of enzyme discovery, engineering, and production. His expertise bridges the crucial gap between enzyme science and its functional applications at the bench, connecting them to a commercially viable path that propels products into the marketplace. Wilson's knowledge base spans computational enzyme design and machine learning, extending through wet-lab molecular biology, protein expression, and assay development. This skill set supports a wide array of operations and research & development efforts, essential for transforming bench science into tangible, market-ready products.
